BGMountains POI icon and type explorer

Locally browsable, based on the original IMG, its extracted 16×16 Garmin TYP icons, and the supplied official Garmin legend. Coordinates are source-decoded, not field verified. v0.6 rejects extended records outside their TRE subdivisions.

Sample coordinate links open a public basemap only; they do not upload map data. Use the Copy control and paste the coordinates into GPXSee showing the original Garmin IMG to verify the mapped symbol and nearby source labels. Known errors and all corrected rules are recorded in the accompanying CSV audit.
